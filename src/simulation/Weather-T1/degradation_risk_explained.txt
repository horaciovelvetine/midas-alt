================================================================================
  DEGRADATION RISK SCORING — HOW IT WORKS & HOW TO USE IT
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
The degradation risk scoring system (Step 6 in the pipeline) converts the
7-day weather forecast into infrastructure risk scores for every Air Force
base. It answers the question: "Given next week's weather, which bases and
which building systems are under the most stress?"

No failure labels are needed. The formulas are based on known physics of how
weather degrades building materials — freeze-thaw cracking concrete, wind
lifting roof membranes, humidity corroding electrical contacts, etc.

The output is designed to plug directly into the MIDAS-alt degradation
simulation as a weather multiplier on facility condition index decay rates.


================================================================================
  HOW TO RUN IT
================================================================================

Option 1: Run the full pipeline (fetches fresh data + predictions + risk)

    python pipeline.py

Option 2: Skip API fetches, reuse cached data (faster)

    python pipeline.py --skip-fetch

Option 3: Run only the risk scoring step (requires existing forecast CSV)

    python code/build_degradation_risk.py

The script reads:
    data/predictions/all_bases_7day_forecast.csv

And produces:
    data/predictions/degradation_risk_scores.csv
    data/predictions/degradation_risk_scores.xlsx
    png/degradation_system_risk_heatmap.png
    png/degradation_top_risk_bases.png

    png/degradation_cluster_risk_comparison.png


================================================================================
  STEP-BY-STEP: HOW THE SCORING WORKS
================================================================================

The process has 5 stages:

  [7-Day Forecast CSV]
        |
        v
  STAGE 1: Aggregate raw weather stressors per base (7-day totals/maxes)
        |
        v
  STAGE 2: Normalize each stressor to a 0-100 component score
        |
        v
  STAGE 3: Weighted combination into 6 system-type risk scores
        |
        v
  STAGE 4: Weighted combination into 1 overall infrastructure risk score
        |
        v
  STAGE 5: Assign risk band (Low / Moderate / High / Critical)
        |
        v
  [Output CSV + Excel + Visualizations]


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 1: RAW WEATHER STRESSORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

For each base, the 7 days of forecast data are aggregated into 11 stressors:

  Stressor                   How It's Calculated
  -------------------------  ---------------------------------------------------
  freeze_thaw_days           Count of days where temp_max > 32F AND temp_min < 32F
  total_precip_mm            Sum of daily precipitation over 7 days
  total_rain_mm              Sum of daily rain over 7 days
  total_snow_cm              Sum of daily snowfall over 7 days
  total_precip_hours         Sum of daily precipitation hours over 7 days
  max_wind_gust_kmh          Maximum single-day wind gust in the 7 days
  avg_wind_speed_kmh         Average daily mean wind speed
  avg_humidity_max_pct       Average of daily max relative humidity
  max_apparent_temp_f        Highest apparent (feels-like) temperature
  avg_temp_swing_f           Average of (daily_max - daily_min) temperature
  max_consecutive_wet_days   Longest streak of days with precipitation > 0
  avg_cloud_cover_pct        Average daily cloud cover percentage


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 2: NORMALIZED COMPONENT SCORES (0 to 100)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each raw stressor is converted to a 0-100 score using thresholds based on
known damage onset points from civil engineering standards.

  Component         Formula                                    Why This Threshold
  ----------------  -----------------------------------------  ---------------------------
  freeze_thaw       min(days / 5 * 100, 100)                   Concrete survives ~300 total
                                                                lifetime cycles. 5/week is
                                                                the maximum practical rate.

  rain              min(precip_mm / 75 * 100, 100)             75mm/week (3 inches) is NWS
                                                                "heavy" — overwhelms drainage,
                                                                drives active water intrusion.

  snow              min(snow_cm / 25 * 100, 100)               25cm/week creates significant
                                                                structural snow load and ice
                                                                dam risk on roofing.

  wind              min(max(0, gust - 20) / 80 * 100, 100)    Below 20 km/h: zero risk.
                                                                100 km/h (62 mph): severe —
                                                                roof membrane failure zone.
                                                                Matches ASCE 7 wind load curve.

  humidity          min(max(0, hum - 50) / 45 * 100, 100)     Below 50% RH: corrosion and
                                                                mold are negligible. Above 70%:
                                                                steel corrosion accelerates.
                                                                95%+: condensation on electronics.

  heat              min(max(0, temp - 85) / 25 * 100, 100)    Below 85F: HVAC operates in spec.
                                                                95F: near-continuous HVAC run.
                                                                110F: asphalt softens, transformers
                                                                overload, compressor failures.

  temp_swing        min(swing / 40 * 100, 100)                40F daily range is extreme thermal
                                                                cycling. Sealant lifespan drops
                                                                ~40% when swings exceed 30F.
                                                                Expansion/contraction fatigues
                                                                joints and connections.

  wetness           min(hours / 48 * 100, 100)                48+ hours of precipitation in a
                                                                week means prolonged moisture
                                                                exposure — materials never dry,
                                                                accelerating rot and corrosion.

  wet_streak        min(consec_days / 5 * 100, 100)           5+ consecutive wet days prevents
                                                                drying between events. Worse
                                                                for some materials than total
                                                                rain volume.

Example: A base with 3 freeze-thaw days, 40mm rain, and 70 km/h gusts:
  freeze_thaw_score = 3/5 * 100       = 60
  rain_score        = 40/75 * 100      = 53.3
  wind_score        = (70-20)/80 * 100 = 62.5


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 3: SYSTEM-TYPE RISK SCORES (6 systems, each 0-100)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Different building systems fail from different weather stressors. Each system
type uses a different weighting based on DOD facility maintenance data
(UFC 3-701-01) and BUILDER SMS failure mode distributions.

ROOFING SYSTEMS
  0.30 x wind  +  0.25 x rain  +  0.20 x freeze_thaw
  + 0.10 x snow  +  0.10 x wetness  +  0.05 x temp_swing
  Why: Wind uplift is the #1 cause of commercial roof failure. Rain tests
  the roof's primary function. Freeze-thaw attacks flashing and seams.

ELECTRICAL / CONTROLS
  0.30 x humidity  +  0.25 x heat  +  0.15 x wet_streak
  + 0.15 x rain  +  0.10 x wind  +  0.05 x temp_swing
  Why: Condensation from humidity shorts circuits and corrodes contacts.
  Heat degrades insulation and overloads transformers.

PAVEMENT / FOUNDATIONS
  0.30 x freeze_thaw  +  0.25 x temp_swing  +  0.20 x rain
  + 0.10 x wetness  +  0.10 x snow  +  0.05 x wind
  Why: Freeze-thaw is the primary mechanism for pothole formation.
  Thermal cycling drives joint deterioration in concrete slabs.

HVAC / MECHANICAL
  0.30 x heat  +  0.25 x temp_swing  +  0.20 x humidity
  + 0.15 x freeze_thaw  +  0.10 x wind
  Why: Extreme heat forces compressors to full capacity (accelerated wear).
  Large temp swings cause frequent heating/cooling mode changes (valve fatigue).

EXTERIOR ENVELOPE (walls, windows, doors)
  0.25 x wind  +  0.25 x rain  +  0.20 x freeze_thaw
  + 0.15 x temp_swing  +  0.10 x humidity  +  0.05 x snow
  Why: Wind-driven rain is the dominant moisture intrusion path for walls.
  Freeze-thaw cracks masonry and degrades sealants around openings.

PLUMBING / WATER SYSTEMS
  0.35 x freeze_thaw  +  0.25 x temp_swing  +  0.20 x rain
  + 0.10 x humidity  +  0.10 x wetness
  Why: Pipe bursts from freezing are catastrophic and costly. Temperature
  cycling fatigues pipe joints and fittings over time.

Example (continuing from above):
  roofing_risk = 0.30*62.5 + 0.25*53.3 + 0.20*60 + 0.10*0 + 0.10*0 + 0.05*0
               = 18.75 + 13.33 + 12.0
               = 44.08

  pavement_risk = 0.30*60 + 0.25*0 + 0.20*53.3 + 0.10*0 + 0.10*0 + 0.05*62.5
                = 18.0 + 10.66 + 3.125
                = 31.79


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 4: OVERALL INFRASTRUCTURE RISK (single 0-100 score per base)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Weighted average across all 6 system types:

  0.25 x roofing
  + 0.20 x pavement
  + 0.20 x electrical
  + 0.15 x hvac
  + 0.10 x envelope
  + 0.10 x plumbing

Roofing and pavement are weighted highest because they represent the largest
share of facility maintenance costs and have the most weather-sensitive
failure modes across DOD installations.

Example (continuing):
  overall_risk = 0.25*44.08 + 0.20*31.79 + ... = ~35 (Moderate)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 5: RISK BANDS AND MIDAS MULTIPLIER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RISK BANDS:
  Score      Band        Meaning
  --------   ---------   ------------------------------------------------
   0 - 25    Low         Normal weather, no acceleration of degradation
  26 - 50    Moderate    Mild stress, schedule preventive inspection
  51 - 75    High        Significant stress, accelerate maintenance
  76 - 100   Critical    Extreme stress, immediate assessment recommended

MIDAS WEATHER MULTIPLIER:
  multiplier = 1.0 + (overall_risk / 100) * 0.5

  This produces a value between 1.0 and 1.5:
    Risk  0  -> multiplier 1.00 (no change to degradation rate)
    Risk 25  -> multiplier 1.125 (12.5% faster degradation)
    Risk 50  -> multiplier 1.25  (25% faster degradation)
    Risk 75  -> multiplier 1.375 (37.5% faster degradation)
    Risk 100 -> multiplier 1.50  (50% faster degradation)


================================================================================
  HOW TO USE WITH MIDAS-ALT
================================================================================

METHOD 1: OVERALL MULTIPLIER (simplest)
----------------------------------------
Apply the single multiplier to each facility's condition index decay:

    weather_data = pd.read_csv("data/predictions/degradation_risk_scores.csv")
    base_row = weather_data[weather_data["base_name"] == installation.name]
    multiplier = base_row["midas_weather_multiplier"].values[0]

    for facility in installation.facilities:
        facility.condition_index -= base_degradation_rate * multiplier


METHOD 2: SYSTEM-SPECIFIC MULTIPLIERS (more granular)
------------------------------------------------------
Use the 6 system-type risk scores to drive per-system degradation:

    for facility in installation.facilities:
        for system in facility.systems:
            system_risk = base_row[f"{system.type}_risk"].values[0]
            system_multiplier = 1.0 + (system_risk / 100) * 0.5
            system.condition_index -= system.base_rate * system_multiplier

This lets MIDAS model situations where a base has low overall risk but
high plumbing risk (freeze-thaw spike) — pipes degrade fast while HVAC
stays normal.


METHOD 3: DEPENDENCY CHAIN INHERITANCE
---------------------------------------
For MIDAS dependency chains, the highest upstream system risk propagates:

    for chain in facility.dependency_chains:
        max_upstream_risk = max(
            base_row[f"{sys.type}_risk"].values[0]
            for sys in chain.upstream_systems
        )
        chain_multiplier = 1.0 + (max_upstream_risk / 100) * 0.5
        chain.downstream.condition_index -= rate * chain_multiplier


METHOD 4: UFC RESILIENCY GRADE MAPPING
---------------------------------------
Map risk bands to UFC resiliency grades for reporting:

    Risk Band    UFC Grade   Action
    ----------   ---------   ------------------------------------------
    Low          G1          No weather-driven maintenance concern
    Moderate     G2          Schedule preventive inspection
    High         G3          Accelerate maintenance timeline
    Critical     G4          Immediate assessment recommended


================================================================================
  OUTPUT FILE COLUMNS
================================================================================

The output CSV/Excel (degradation_risk_scores.csv) contains one row per base:

IDENTIFICATION:
  base_name                  Name of the Air Force base
  cluster_id                 Weather cluster (0=Warm, 1=Cool, 2=Cold & Wet)
  latitude                   Base latitude
  longitude                  Base longitude
  forecast_start             First day of the 7-day forecast window
  forecast_end               Last day of the 7-day forecast window

RAW STRESSORS:
  freeze_thaw_days           Number of freeze-thaw cycle days (0-7)
  total_precip_mm            Total precipitation in millimeters
  total_rain_mm              Total rain in millimeters
  total_snow_cm              Total snowfall in centimeters
  total_precip_hours         Total hours of precipitation
  max_wind_gust_kmh          Peak wind gust in km/h
  avg_wind_speed_kmh         Average wind speed in km/h
  avg_humidity_max_pct       Average daily max humidity (%)
  max_apparent_temp_f        Peak feels-like temperature (F)
  avg_temp_swing_f           Average daily temp range (F)
  max_consecutive_wet_days   Longest wet streak (days)
  avg_cloud_cover_pct        Average cloud cover (%)

COMPONENT SCORES (0-100):
  freeze_thaw_score          Freeze-thaw stress intensity
  rain_score                 Precipitation stress intensity
  snow_score                 Snow load stress intensity
  wind_score                 Wind damage stress intensity
  humidity_score             Humidity/corrosion stress intensity
  heat_score                 Heat stress intensity
  temp_swing_score           Thermal cycling stress intensity
  wetness_score              Prolonged moisture stress intensity
  wet_streak_score           Consecutive wet day stress intensity

SYSTEM RISK SCORES (0-100):
  roofing_risk               Roofing system risk
  electrical_risk            Electrical / controls risk
  pavement_risk              Pavement / foundations risk
  hvac_risk                  HVAC / mechanical risk
  envelope_risk              Exterior envelope risk
  plumbing_risk              Plumbing / water systems risk

FINAL SCORES:
  overall_risk               Weighted overall infrastructure risk (0-100)
  risk_band                  Low / Moderate / High / Critical
  midas_weather_multiplier   Ready-to-use multiplier for MIDAS (1.0-1.5)

================================================================================
  FUTURE UPGRADES
================================================================================

Once MIDAS generates actual degradation outcomes (condition index drops
correlated with dates), the fixed formula weights can be replaced with
learned weights:

  1. Export weekly CI drops from MIDAS per facility per system type
  2. Pair each week's CI drop with that week's 9 component scores
  3. Run linear regression: CI_drop ~ w1*freeze_thaw + w2*rain + ...
  4. Replace the hardcoded weights in SYSTEM_WEIGHTS with learned ones

The scoring pipeline stays identical — only the weight constants change.
This turns the rule-based MVP into a data-driven model with zero
architectural changes.
